River Crossing Rules

Help the farmer move the Wolf, the Sheep, and the Cabbage from the Start Bank to the Destination Bank!

The boat carries the Farmer and up to ONE item.
When left alone on either bank without the farmer:
  • The Wolf will eat the Sheep!
  • The Sheep will eat the Cabbage!
Need a hint? Click for step-by-step solution
  1. Cross with Sheep to Destination $\rightarrow$ Return alone
  2. Cross with Wolf $\rightarrow$ Bring Sheep back to Start!
  3. Cross with Cabbage to Destination $\rightarrow$ Return alone
  4. Cross with Sheep to Destination $\rightarrow$ Win!
